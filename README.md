# BusMate 🚌

**BusMate** is a real-time bus tracking application designed for college students.
The app helps students know exactly where their college bus is, reducing wait times and uncertainty.
Unlike traditional GPS-enabled systems, BusMate relies on **crowdsourced location sharing** — students inside the bus share their live location, which updates the bus’s current position for everyone.

---

## 🚀 Features

* **Live Bus Tracking** – See the exact location of your bus in real-time.
* **Crowdsourced Data** – Accuracy improves as more students on the bus share their location.
* **Minimal Battery Usage** – Lightweight location updates optimized for mobile.
* **Bus Route Overview** – View all college bus routes and stops.
* **Arrival Estimates** – Get an ETA for your stop.
* **Privacy First** – Location shared only when a user is inside the bus and app is active.

---

## 📱 How It Works

1. A student on the bus opens BusMate and shares their live location.
2. The location gets broadcasted as the “bus position.”
3. Other students waiting for the bus see real-time updates on their app.
4. Once the student leaves the bus or stops sharing, the system automatically looks for the next active sharer.
